## Configuration intent

Searchers often specify the visual configuration rather than the brand. Where those products genuinely exist, preserve the exact configuration in title/H1/schema/alt text and internal links:
- one-hole / full-face opening
- three-hole
- five-hole
- bunny/rabbit ears
- camo pattern
- exact color
- pack/bundle quantity

Do not collapse distinct configurations into a generic `ski mask` page if the products have materially different appearances.

Editorial/style pages should internally link to the exact relevant product/configuration and use real product photography.

## Page strategy

1. Exact PDPs own transactional product + color/configuration intent.
2. A balaclava collection/category page owns broad `balaclava ski mask`, `streetwear balaclava`, and style browsing.
3. Bunny-ear collection/editorial content owns bunny/rabbit/kawaii intent if supported by current products.
4. Camo collection/editorial content owns camo/camouflage intent.
5. Style/editorial content supports outfit queries; do not generate one page per trivial keyword permutation.
