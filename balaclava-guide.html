Balaclava Configuration Guide

1-Hole Balaclava

A 1-hole balaclava features a single wide opening that exposes both the eyes and the nose/mouth area together. This provides maximum visibility and breathability while covering the neck, head, and sides of the face.

3-Hole Balaclava

A 3-hole balaclava features distinct, individual openings for each eye and a third opening for the mouth. The nose bridge and cheeks remain fully covered, providing more facial insulation than a 1-hole configuration.

5-Hole Balaclava

A 5-hole balaclava provides separate small openings for each eye, each nostril, and the mouth. This maximizes facial coverage and insulation while still allowing for breathing and visibility. It is the most comprehensive coverage option.
