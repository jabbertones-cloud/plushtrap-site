Balaclava Configuration Guide

This guide describes configuration by the number and placement of face openings. Individual product pages remain the authority for a specific item's materials, dimensions, availability, and other product details.

1-Hole Balaclava

A 1-hole configuration uses one shared face opening rather than separate openings for the eyes and mouth.

3-Hole Balaclava

A 3-hole configuration uses two eye openings and one mouth opening.

5-Hole Balaclava

A 5-hole configuration uses two eye openings, two nostril openings, and one mouth opening.
